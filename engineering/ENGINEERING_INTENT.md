# Engineering Intent

## Mission

Define and automate reproducible infrastructure through Infrastructure
as Code practices.

## Core Engineering Intent

-   Standardize infrastructure provisioning.
-   Reduce configuration drift.
-   Promote repeatable deployments.
-   Capture infrastructure knowledge as code.

## Engineering Principles

-   Prefer evolutionary improvement over redesign.
-   Treat engineering knowledge as a first-class organizational asset.
-   Preserve architectural consistency across the project portfolio.
-   Record durable engineering decisions explicitly.

## Non-Goals

-   Replace application deployment pipelines.
-   Manage business application logic.
