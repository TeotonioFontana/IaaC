# Engineering Program

## Objective

Guide the engineering evolution of the project through disciplined,
incremental improvements.

## Core Activities

-   Capture infrastructure knowledge.
-   Standardize provisioning.
-   Promote reusable infrastructure components.

## Common Engineering Rules

-   Engineering knowledge is a first-class organizational asset.
-   Follow the corporate Engineering folder standard.
-   Prefer evolutionary improvement over redesign.
-   Record durable engineering decisions explicitly.
-   Keep the EKB as the canonical engineering reference.
